ChessCam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ChessCam (the website at www.chesscam.net and the Android app com.camerachess.www.twa, which opens the same website) recognises chess moves from your camera. It is open source, and you can check everything described here in the code at github.com/Pbatch/CameraChessWeb.

ChessCam has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Camera and videos

ChessCam asks for camera access so it can watch your chess board. Video from the camera, and any video file you upload, is processed entirely on your device. It is never recorded, uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.

Data stored on your device

ChessCam stores the following in your browser's local storage, so it survives closing the app:

  • the current game (its moves and positions);
  • if you log in to Lichess, your Lichess username and access token.

This data stays on your device. You can delete it by logging out, or by clearing the site's data in your browser or app settings.

Lichess

Logging in to Lichess is optional and only needed to play, broadcast or export games there. Login uses Lichess's own OAuth page; ChessCam never sees your Lichess password. ChessCam asks for permission to:

  • read and write your studies (study:read, study:write), to export and broadcast games;
  • play games with the Board API (board:play), to play online games on a physical board.

When you use these features, ChessCam sends your moves and games directly from your device to Lichess. Logging out of ChessCam revokes the token. You can also revoke it at any time at lichess.org/account/security. Lichess's handling of your data is covered by the Lichess privacy policy.

Downloads from other services

To work, ChessCam downloads files from these services. Like any website, they receive your IP address and browser details when they send you a file:

  • Amazon Web Services (S3 and CloudFront) hosts www.chesscam.net;
  • Hugging Face hosts the chess recognition model, which is downloaded once and then cached on your device;
  • jsDelivr serves the app's icons.

No personal data or video is sent to any of them.

Children

ChessCam does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. Lichess accounts are subject to Lichess's own age requirements.
